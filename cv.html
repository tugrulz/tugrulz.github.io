Tuğrulcan Elmas

Assistant Professor, School of Informatics, University of Edinburgh

tugrulcanelmas at gmail · tugrulcan.com · Google Scholar · LinkedIn · GitHub · YouTube

AI researcher and educator working on large language models, natural language processing, and AI-generated media detection. I teach information retrieval, NLP and computational social science at the University of Edinburgh — ranked 22nd in the world for Computer Science & Information Systems (QS World University Rankings by Subject) — and I have built AI curricula for university students, summer institutes, and a general public audience. I teach and lecture in both English and Turkish.
